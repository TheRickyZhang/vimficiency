== 1. Correctness and plan quality

1. Triage the large PlanRegret regrets. Real cost is ~5x the planner estimate
   on 7-region plans, which is too large to be partition quality; something in
   composition breaks down or misses a case. Start from DumpCase. [arch 7]

2. Hand composition the planner's tied plans instead of one arbitrary pick.
   Needs a small tied-set surface on VimDiff again. [arch 0]

3. Pure-newline deletion ("\n" -> "") cannot fire single-step structural edits.

4. Linewise deletes other than dd are classified EntryCurswant::Required,
   although Neovim's dj follows curswant like dd. Classify them Followed after
   an oracle check, so they stay usable from a clamped column.

5. J/gJ do not model comment-leader stripping ('comments', formatoptions+=j).
   The oracle clears 'comments' to agree, so real buffers can diverge.

6. Sentence text objects are ported but held back behind the backward
   findSentenceForOperator gap.


== 2. Search cost

7. Check startWorkFactor (8) on large real regions. It was tuned on generated
   2-4 line deletions only; a start more than 8x harder than the rest loses
   its result. See dev/optimizer/transform-optimizer.md.

8. Give later-line starts a backward route when the region's first line is
   whitespace only after a prefix. Today those starts have no solution.

9. Share Transform search states across start positions. [arch 11]

10. Replace composition's up-to-8 single-point nav searches per pop with one
    range search filtered against startPositions(). Measure before and after.
    [arch 7]

11. Tune the VimDiff planner cell cap (100M cells at ~48 B/cell is likely
    10-20x too high for memory), and the seal gate threshold, which scales with
    paragraph depth. Tightening the seal gate needs a dominance proof.

12. Measure the diff-generation vs composition time split on realistic slices
    and decide whether planner exactness is buying anything. [arch 7]
